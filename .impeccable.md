## Design Context

### Users

Seymour is for software and infrastructure engineers who understand programming and LLM concepts but are new to performance computing and inference systems. They use it to build an accurate mental model of how transformer operations become kernels, how tensors cross hardware boundaries, why bottlenecks move, and where optimizations at the model, runtime, kernel, and system layers can act.

### Brand Personality

Authoritative, mechanical, and generous. Seymour should feel like an expert engineer teaching at a physical museum exhibit: precise without being academic, opinionated without being obscure, and memorable without becoming a toy. The Little Shop of Horrors feeding metaphor supplies personality, but never outranks the technical explanation.

### Aesthetic Direction

A calm industrial teaching instrument. Use the existing warm paper, deep green, muted mustard, and restrained red as an annotated engineering-plate palette. Hardware stays spatially stable. Color identifies data classes and the current causal path rather than decorating the page. The primary experience is light and legible for sustained learning; dark green is reserved for focused technical sections. Avoid dashboard-card sprawl, constant motion, neon observability aesthetics, and unexplained jargon.

### Design Principles

1. Show causality in one view: algorithm → tensors → kernel → hardware path → bottleneck → optimization.
2. Keep the map stable. Motion is user-triggered feedback, never a default ambient cycle.
3. Explain terminology where it appears, with vendor fact, Seymour assumption, and computed result clearly distinguished.
4. Progressive disclosure: lead with an intelligible system path, then reveal shapes, schedules, lanes, and formulas without forcing a long scroll.
5. Demonstrate expertise through tradeoffs. Every optimization must say what boundary it changes, why it helps, and when it does not.
6. Preserve personality through the feeding metaphor and print-like visual language, but let the engineering model remain primary.
